# EXPRESS

### Real-World Social Friends

> **Your Journey. Your People. Your Connections.**

EXPRESS is a social-first journey app. The first MVP is focused on trains: useful railway journey tools bring people into the product, while the core experience helps them discover and connect with people sharing the same journey.

## MVP

- 🎫 PNR check — private journey utility
- 🚆 Live / running train status
- 👥 People on your train
- 👋 Say Hi → Accept / Ignore
- 💬 Chat after mutual acceptance
- ❤️ Stay Connected after the journey
- 🔒 Privacy-first discoverability and journey expiry

## Product principle

**PNR and train status are the utility layer. Human connection is the product.**

We do not position EXPRESS as a nearby-strangers or dating-first app. The core context is **people sharing your journey**.

## Future-ready model

The train is the first context, not the final category:

`User → Context → Journey/Event/Place → People → Intent → Connection → Conversation → Relationship`

Future contexts can include campus, concerts, airports, stadiums, conferences and other real-world moments without redesigning the product around a single vertical.

## Design direction

- Premium, friendly, trustworthy
- Blue-led travel identity with green live-status cues
- Mobile-first responsive UI
- Social discovery is visually prominent
- Profiles are visible before a request
- No chat until mutual acceptance
- Exact seat/coach and PNR are never exposed to other users

## Current build

The initial UI is a responsive Next.js experience centered on the **Home / Journey** screen and the **People on your train** discovery flow. Interactive profile preview and Say Hi states are included as the foundation for the next screens.
